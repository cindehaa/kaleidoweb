# Benchmark Suite

Seven demanding subjects with deliberately different informational structures. These are the permanent evaluation suite. A system that produces seven superficially different landing pages has failed; each subject should make it obvious *why* it got its particular experience.

| # | Subject | Wikipedia article | Informational structure | Presentation hypotheses (to be beaten) |
|---|---------|-------------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | Historical event | Apollo 11 | Chronology + engineering + human drama + imagery | Mission clock as spine; trajectory as spatial narrative; radio transcript moments |
| 2 | Scientific mechanism | Photosynthesis | Nested process, flows, scales (leaf → molecule) | Zoom-through-scales; animated flow diagrams; light/dark reactions as parallel tracks |
| 3 | Biography | Ada Lovelace | Life arc, relationships, ideas ahead of their time | Correspondence & ideas as primary objects, not dates; relationship constellation |
| 4 | Mathematical concept | Fourier transform | Abstraction, equivalence, decomposition | Interactive decomposition (draw a wave → see components); epicycles |
| 5 | Geography-heavy | Silk Road | Routes, exchange, places, longue durée | Journey along a map; goods/ideas as travelers; place-anchored stories |
| 6 | Art / culture | Hokusai (The Great Wave) | Imagery, technique, career, influence | The artwork as the interface; zoomable print; woodblock process layers |
| 7 | Engineering object | Golden Gate Bridge | Form, forces, dimensions, construction, place | Structural forces made visible; scale comparisons; construction sequence |

## First vertical slice

**Apollo 11.** Richest combination of chronology, engineering, imagery (public domain NASA), quotation (real transcripts), and drama. Best chance to prove the ceiling.

## Latency metrics (measured per benchmark, once pipeline exists)

- button → visible reaction (target < 200 ms)
- button → useful transformed content (target < 3 s)
- button → coherent initial experience (target < 10 s)
- button → fully enhanced experience (target < 60 s, streaming in)
